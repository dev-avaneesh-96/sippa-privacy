Sippa Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short

Sippa keeps your information on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and Sippa does not upload or share your data.

What Sippa stores

Your name, gender, age range and weight, which you enter when you first open the app. You can change them in Settings.

Your water entries: the amount, date and time of each glass you log.

Your settings: daily goal, glass size, quick-add sizes, reminder times and interval, units (ml or oz, kg or lb) and your theme choice.

All of this sits in a private database inside the app’s own storage on your phone.

How it is used

Your name is used for the greeting on the Home screen.

Your water entries and goal are used to show your progress, calendar, averages and the home-screen widget.

Your age range and weight are used to suggest a daily water goal. Your gender is stored with your profile but is not used for any calculation. None of this is ever shared.

Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself and need no internet connection.

Permissions

Notifications: to show your drink-water reminders. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android’s notification settings.

Receive boot completed: so reminders start again after the phone restarts.

Health Connect (optional): only if you turn on sync in Settings, so Sippa can write your drinks there. Sippa never reads anything from Health Connect.

Home-screen widget

The widget reads today’s total from the same on-device database. Its Add and Undo buttons change that data on your phone only.

Health Connect (optional)

Off by default. If you turn it on, each drink you log is written to Android’s Health Connect as a hydration record, and removed there if you delete it in Sippa. Other health apps you allow in Health Connect can then use it.

You can turn this off in Sippa or remove access in Health Connect at any time. Records already in Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect.

Backup and export

Sippa creates a backup or CSV file only when you ask for one. The file holds your entries and, for a backup, your profile and settings.

It leaves your phone only through the share sheet you choose, so you decide where it goes. Keep backup files somewhere private.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any water entry inside the app, and change your profile in Settings.

To erase everything, open Android Settings, then Apps, then Sippa, then Storage, and choose Clear data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
